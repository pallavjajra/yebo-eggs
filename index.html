Our Heritage

About Yebo Eggs

Yebo Eggs is a proud family-run business, built on strong values of honesty, quality, and community. We are owner-operated, which means we are hands-on every day — ensuring that every customer receives the best possible service.

At Yebo Eggs, customer care is at the heart of everything we do. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients by offering friendly service, reliability, and real value.

We source our products fresh and directly from suppliers, cutting out the middleman. This allows us to offer some of the best prices in the market while maintaining high quality.

We operate on very low margins, because we understand that times are tough. Our goal is simple — to support our communities by making everyday essentials more affordable.

Any discounts or savings we receive are passed directly back to our customers, ensuring you always get the best deal.
